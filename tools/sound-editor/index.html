HC Sound Editor

Sampled sounds for HyperCard IIGS and the HC Browser, in the IIGS's own format. Everything runs in this page; nothing is uploaded.

Sound

Drag across the waveform to select part of it.

Pitch

HyperCard plays a sound at middle C unless the script names notes. Tell it what pitch the sound already has, and play "name" "c e g" will play the right notes. A sound with no definite pitch (a door, a voice) uses "none".

Sounds in a HyperCard IIGS stack

    
Sounds are resources of type $8024 in a stack's (or HyperCard's) resource fork. Use CiderPress II as with icons: cp2 extract --preserve=naps, open the file ending in r, put it back with cp2 add --from-naps. A script plays one by name: play "Boing".
